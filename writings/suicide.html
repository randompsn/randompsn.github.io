On Suicide

Just one moment, I shall make one decision.
Since then all fallacious circular reasoning,
impure phony,
human+system=politics=>annoyance and
restraints,
they vanish
just like equal ingredients
cancel out
with my life and existence

— all this time, all this time, they have been objects which exist or die with my will!
The joy radiates inside me
just like all children
who have invented their own mathematics:
we know something about the world, and we can use it, we can manipulate it while others are asleep.

That's it,
my life is mine,
and I end it to my will; even,
in the world of livings, if they went nitpicking, finding faults in my real self
thereby stripping my right of speech,
at the very least they no longer nor do they deserve to dictate over my life,
and cease selfishly forcing me to live out of their concern on “societal impacts”.

I shall freely exact my revenge on them with this death!
